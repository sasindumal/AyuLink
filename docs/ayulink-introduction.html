Introduction

What AyuLink does

For patients

  • A personal health assistant Ayu lives on the home screen and keeps the health record current.
  • AI symptom analysis & doctor channelling By voice or chat, in Sinhala or English — ending in a booked appointment.
  • Past health history in natural language Ayu asks in the patient's own words; the structured profile fills itself.
  • Course tracking & reminders Every dose, on schedule, until the full course is complete.

In the consulting room

  • QR-based digital prescription management Scan the Medical ID, read the history, issue the prescription — no paper slip.
  • Feedback collected during the course Returned to the prescribing doctor — and, when recovery stalls, used to point the patient to a better-suited speciality.

Across the network

  • Doctor rating & ranking From real patients, after real treatment — and it feeds the search that finds them.
  • AI-powered pharmacy drug management Verify against what the doctor actually wrote, then dispense item by item.
  • AI-powered channelling-centre appointments Sessions, availability, confirmations and completions in one place.

Challenges

Built around the regulation

AyuLink handles national identity, clinical records and prescriptions. The regulatory surface — not the software — is the hard part, and each instrument below is still work to be done.

Personal Data Protection ActPDPA
A lawful basis, explicit consent and purpose limitation for every health record we hold — plus data-subject rights we must be able to honour on request.

What a remote consultation may and may not conclude, and how the clinician on the other side must be identified and accountable.

Electronic Transactions ActETA
What makes a digital prescription legally equivalent to a signed paper one, and how that equivalence is evidenced later.
To build
NMRA ActNational Medicines Regulatory Authority
How medicines may be prescribed, dispensed and recorded — and what a pharmacy must verify before it hands anything over.

Demo · 01

Ayu takes the history

Ayu asks only for what the profile is still missing, in the patient's own language, and writes the answers back as structured records — never as loose free text a doctor has to interpret.

  • Speaks Sinhala or English — stores English only
  • Plans the interview from what the profile actually lacks
  • Allergies, conditions, medicines, surgeries, family history, lifestyle
  • An explicit I don't know is recorded as unknown, never as "none"

Demo · 02

Symptoms in, appointment out

The patient describes the problem by voice or text. The assistant grounds it against a real medical knowledge graph, explains the likely condition, finds a doctor who treats it, and books the slot.

  • Symptom → disease → speciality, every hop from the graph
  • Never invents a condition the graph doesn't contain
  • Doctors ranked by patient rating, with live availability
  • Ends with a confirmed booking — APT-000123

Demo · 03

The same care, in Sinhala

A patient who is more comfortable in Sinhala never has to switch language. They describe the problem in their own words, and the assistant asks, explains and books in Sinhala too.

මට දවස් දෙකක ඉඳන් උණයි, ඇඟ රිදෙනවා "I've had a fever for two days, and my body aches"

  • Sinhala in, Sinhala out — typed or spoken
  • Follow-up questions and the explanation arrive in Sinhala
  • Same knowledge graph, same doctors, same booking flow

Demo · 04

Scan, read, prescribe

The doctor scans the patient's Medical ID and the clinical history opens — allergies first. The prescription is issued straight to that ID, where the patient and their pharmacy can both read it.

  • Scan the QR, or type AYU-200012345678
  • Allergies surface first, ordered by severity
  • Each entry badged self-reported or clinician-confirmed
  • Structured prescription, dosage schedule and follow-up plan

Demo · 05

Verify, then dispense

The pharmacist scans the same Medical ID — or a single prescription's own QR, so the patient can show just one — and dispenses item by item against exactly what the doctor wrote.

  • No handwriting to decipher, no slip to doubt
  • Item by item, not all-or-nothing
  • A 15-minute undo window on every dispense
  • The patient's care timeline updates the moment it's done
